## Root cause and retained candidate

The candidate adds Long/Byte validation to shared forward/backward decomposition helpers and canonicalizes Byte to Long in regular NLL forward/backward wrappers. Spatial NLL2d Byte rejection remains intact, matching eager rather than broadening the API. Casting before masking also avoids negative ignore-index wrap for unsigned targets. No unrelated component is patched.

New tests cover both compiled backends, ranks 1-4, all five dtypes, accepted forward/gradients, spatial Byte rejection, weighted none/sum/mean reductions, positive ignored Byte labels, negative-ignore no-wrap, and probability targets with smoothing. Actual eager diagnostics across shapes were checked; invalid spatial int32/int16/bool use the asserted target-dtype error family, while spatial Byte reaches the separate Long-scalar diagnostic.
